About Kestrel Line

Kestrel Line Plumbing & Mechanical, LLC was founded in 2009 in Dallas, Texas by Marcus Delgado, a service-disabled U.S. Navy veteran who spent nine years as a Seabee utilitiesman running steam plants and water distribution systems. Marcus owns 100% of the company and runs it day to day.

Where we work

Headquarters and main fabrication shop: Dallas, Texas (Mesquite). Regional offices with their own crews and warehouses: San Antonio, Texas; Atlanta, Georgia; Phoenix, Arizona; and Columbus, Ohio. We hold active plumbing contractor licenses in 24 states and travel self-performing crews anywhere in the continental United States. For remote sites we mobilize a crew within 10 working days of notice to proceed.

How we work

We self-perform all plumbing and pipefitting labor with our own employees. We subcontract only asbestos and lead abatement, excavation over 12 feet deep, and controls programming. Every job is tracked in ServiceTitan, from estimate to closeout, including every asset we touch.
