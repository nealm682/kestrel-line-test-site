Commercial, institutional and federal plumbing and steam systems, nationwide

Kestrel Line Plumbing & Mechanical is the industry-leading partner for hospitals, campuses and government buildings that cannot afford downtime. Our unmatched quality and fully certified crews deliver world-class results on every job.

Since 2009 we have grown from a three-truck Dallas shop into a national self-performing plumbing and mechanical contractor with 168 employees, five offices and active contractor licenses in 24 states. We do commercial, institutional and industrial work only, no residential service calls.

What we do

  • Commercial and institutional plumbing: new construction, renovation and occupied-building retrofits
  • Steam and condensate systems, including full steam trap programs with wireless monitoring
  • Boilers, commercial water heaters and domestic hot water systems
  • Domestic water repiping, sanitary, storm and sewer, including trenchless pipe bursting
  • Medical gas piping (NFPA 99) in hospitals and clinics
  • Laboratory, acid waste and process piping
  • Backflow testing, repair and certification; Legionella water management support
  • Preventive maintenance contracts and 24/7 emergency service

Who we work for

Department of Veterans Affairs medical centers, the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, the General Services Administration, public universities, school districts and private hospital systems. About 55% of our revenue comes from federal prime contracts, most of them as a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business.
